Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 1, 2026

To request deletion of data processed by Gexa, send a deletion request to the Gexa administrator with:

  • Your business name.
  • Your WhatsApp Business Account ID, if available.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number ID, if available.
  • The email or user account used to access Gexa.

Gexa will review the request and delete or anonymize related conversation, contact, and channel data unless retention is required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact: gerencia@gexa.com.co
