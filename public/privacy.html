Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Gexa is a messaging and CRM tool that helps businesses receive, organize, and respond to customer conversations from channels such as WhatsApp.

Information we process

  • Business account identifiers, such as WhatsApp Business Account IDs and phone number IDs.
  • Customer conversation data received through webhooks, including sender identifiers, message text, timestamps, and delivery metadata.
  • Operational logs needed to debug webhook delivery and message processing.

How we use information

  • To display conversations in the Gexa dashboard.
  • To send replies requested by authorized business users.
  • To troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service.

Data sharing

Gexa does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to run the service, including Vercel and Supabase. WhatsApp message sending and receiving is processed through Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.

Data deletion

To request deletion of data related to your business or account, visit /data-deletion or contact the Gexa administrator.
